What I build: ASRS software

daifuku · intralogistics

At Daifuku I work on the software layer that makes automated warehouses run, connecting business logic, inventory systems and physical material-handling equipment.

An ASRS stores and retrieves loads from high-density racks automatically, using stacker cranes, conveyors, STVs and robot arms, all coordinated by software:

  • Storage racks: high-bay structures for dense inventory
  • Stacker cranes & shuttles: put away and pick loads
  • Conveyors & STVs: move loads between zones
  • WMS & WCS software: the logic and real-time control
